    What the stratum is, and how much it can carry

    Under the plaza runs a single lane, one storey down: the stratum. Deliveries arrive at a loading dock on the street, one lift lowers them, and small robots carry them along the lane to four buildings. The charts below come from the same simulation as the map, run thousands of times.

    1 · The journey of one package

    Left to right: what happens, how long it takes, and how many per hour each step can handle. The narrowest step sets the pace for everything.

    2 · How much per day

    Carts delivered in a 16-hour operating day, for each way of running the lane. The solid bar is what runs without carts spilling onto the curb; the pale extension is the most it can carry before the dock queue grows without end.

    3 · Intermediate scales of operation

    From one building to the full block and beyond: carts per day at each step up, and what each step needs.

    4 · Can it run through floor plenums?

    Heights drawn to scale. A plenum is the void under a raised floor.

    5 · Can it swarm?

    How many robots can be in the lane at once, and whether they jam.
